# Scene consistency review

Review Candidate Observations against explicit SceneSpec, SceneState and SceneBinding expectations.

Check:

- geography and zone connections;
- entrance/exit orientation;
- landmark and occlusion placement;
- architecture construction and support;
- scale anchors and perspective;
- material identity, aging and light response;
- fixed/movable prop continuity;
- motivated light and shadow direction;
- weather, atmosphere and particle direction;
- camera axis, side, eyeline and screen direction;
- rights and reference scope.

Record expected, observed, status, severity and Observation evidence. Unknown evidence remains unknown.
