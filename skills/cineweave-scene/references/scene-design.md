# Scene design

A SceneSpec is a reusable spatial fact model, not a background prompt.

## Design order

1. Narrative function and audience experience.
2. Orientation and immutable geography anchors.
3. Zones, connections, entrances and exits.
4. Scale anchors tied to people, known objects or construction intervals.
5. Architecture: function, placement, silhouette, support and construction.
6. Material system: surface, construction, age, weathering and light response.
7. Prop layout: function, zone, placement, mobility and continuity priority.
8. Camera topology: safe axes, camera anchors, occlusion anchors and forbidden positions.
9. Controllable scene variables, reference policy, rights and validation.

## Anti-patterns

- “huge palace” without scale anchors;
- architecture floating without structural support;
- entrances or landmarks changing between shots;
- global mood filters replacing motivated light;
- style references overriding geography;
- weather states silently changing construction or topography.
