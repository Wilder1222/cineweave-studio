# Scene review and repair

A SceneReview diagnoses. A SceneRepair proposes one controlled change.

## Review decision

Choose accept, repair, reject or collect more evidence. A blocking geography, scale or axis failure prevents acceptance even when lighting and materials look strong.

## Repair contract

- bind the exact SceneSpec and parent Candidate Observation;
- cite one failure and confidence;
- preserve all passed character, camera, material and geography items;
- change one target path;
- forbid whole-scene regeneration when a local semantic repair is sufficient;
- state acceptance checks, stop condition and human approval gate;
- do not claim success before the repaired Candidate is verified.
