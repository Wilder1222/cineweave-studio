# Domain prompt recipes

Use these as routing checklists, not fixed templates.

## Portrait and character

Prioritize visible structural anchors, age, gaze, current expression, posture, hair–skin contact, wardrobe silhouette, skin-zone response and lens distance. Add body proportions or muscle definition only for a framing that shows them. Separate identity from makeup, costume and render style.

## Product and food

Define product geometry, label-facing orientation, support surface, scale cue, contact shadow, key/fill/reflection cards, material roughness, condensation or food state, and commercial crop. Never invent brand text.

## Architecture and interior

Define cultural/temporal context and whether it is researched reconstruction or inspired reinterpretation; then typology, site, massing, structural rhythm, circulation, openings, material assembly, wear, human scale, weather and camera position. Ornament comes after structure.

## Landscape

Define terrain hierarchy, weather system, time, foreground–midground–background scale, atmospheric perspective, dominant directional light and a scale anchor. Avoid treating fog as a substitute for geography.

## Illustration, comics and animation

Define medium, anatomy abstraction, line behavior, shape language, value grouping, palette logic, surface/brush treatment and spatial convention. For video, line stability and temporal style belong in a separate temporal contract.

## Fashion and editorial

Define garment silhouette, construction, drape, material response, styling hierarchy, body pose, editorial viewpoint, backdrop, light and intended publication crop. Do not let accessories overwhelm the garment's structural read.
