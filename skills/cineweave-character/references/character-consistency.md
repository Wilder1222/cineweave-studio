# Character consistency review

Review supplied Candidate Observations against explicit expectations.

## Tests

- recognizable without wardrobe;
- recognizable with hair obscured;
- black silhouette remains distinct;
- face anchors survive emotion changes;
- head-to-body and shoulder/hip relations survive camera changes;
- dominant side, prop side and occupational traces remain coherent;
- motion fingerprint survives shot changes;
- appearance state changes do not mutate identity;
- CharacterBinding start/end state continues across adjacent shots;
- all identity references have an explicit role, scope and rights status.

## Evidence discipline

Each criterion records expected, observed, status, severity and Observation IDs. `unknown` means evidence is insufficient; do not convert it to pass. A blocking failure prevents acceptance even when the image is aesthetically strong.
