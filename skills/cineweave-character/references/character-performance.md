# Character performance

Performance is a shot state derived from, but not written back into, CharacterSpec identity.

## Binding formula

`baseline + objective + obstacle + emotion + intensity + concealment + trigger + action arc = CharacterBinding`

## Observable channels

Every binding should make these observable when relevant:

- posture and center of gravity;
- gaze target and eye/head/body order;
- face tension rather than only an emotion word;
- left/right hand function and dominant side;
- breath pause, rhythm or release;
- voice volume, tempo and sentence shape;
- start, trigger, peak and end state.

## Shot-scale compilation

- close-up: face geometry, surface, asymmetry, gaze, breath and micro-expression;
- medium: face anchors, shoulder/neck posture, hands and weight transfer;
- full body: silhouette, proportion, center of gravity, dominant side and action mechanics;
- wide: silhouette, screen direction, action readability and costume outline;
- silhouette: contour, weight, gesture and direction only.

Do not dump the full CharacterSpec into every prompt. Select only visible anchors and required continuity facts.

## Emotional restraint

An emotion label is insufficient. “Fear” may become a foot turning toward an exit, a breath interruption and reduced blinking while the upper body remains stable. Concealment controls how much of the internal state leaks into the observable channels.


## Behavior causality and emotion vector

Before performance cues, state perception, appraisal, chosen strategy, suppressed impulse and why the final action solves the character objective under the current obstacle. Emotion control includes primary and secondary emotions, intensity, concealment, valence, arousal, dominance, trajectory and leakage channels. Micro-expression separates eyes, brows, mouth, jaw and timing. Personality is compiled into these observable decisions; it is never passed as an unsupported label alone.
