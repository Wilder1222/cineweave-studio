# Story continuity

Store facts as version-bound assertions, not memory summaries.

## Ledger entries

Each entry identifies entity, fact path, scalar value, exact source refs, validity interval and status. Useful domains include:

- character knowledge, injury, possession and relationship;
- prop location and ownership;
- setup/payoff state;
- location and travel time;
- promises, secrets and revealed information;
- world rules and exceptions.

When two active entries disagree, create a visible conflict with severity and resolution status. Do not choose the newest, most convenient or most dramatic value without an explicit decision.

## Continuity is not sameness

A deliberate change is valid when its cause and transition are recorded. The ledger protects causal history; it does not freeze all variables. Distinguish `changed` from `broken`, and distinguish an accepted variation from a resolved contradiction.

Before handing off a scene, check what every participant knows, carries, wants and can physically access at that time.
