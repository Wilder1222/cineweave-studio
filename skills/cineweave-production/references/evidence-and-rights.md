# Evidence and rights

An EvidenceBundle separates face identity, body identity, full character, makeup, hair, costume, material, expression, pose, motion, depth, segmentation, mask, composition, light and scene geography.

Each evidence item must include one Observation ID, one role, one scope, preserve and excluded uses, quality/confidence, target contract refs and a LicenseProfile. A face portrait cannot serve as body proportion or costume construction evidence. A style image cannot replace identity or geography.

LicenseProfile records code, weight, base-model, dependency, dataset, reference-asset and identity rights separately. Open source does not imply commercial use. Unknown, conflicting or denied rights block final execution when the affected input is required.
