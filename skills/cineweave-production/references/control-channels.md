# Control channels

Every controllable requirement must declare target, source, enforcement, priority, preserve contract, allowed deviation, adapter requirements and fallback.

- `hard`: identity, body proportion, locked appearance, geography, required contact, rights and exact continuity. Failure blocks.
- `soft`: intended expression, pose, light balance or material response that may tolerate bounded deviation and review.
- `advisory`: style preference and optional visual language. It may degrade but cannot override higher-priority facts.

Default conflict order:

1. World Canon and rights;
2. CharacterSpec and SceneSpec;
3. AppearanceState and SceneState;
4. CharacterBinding, SceneBinding and InteractionConstraintSet;
5. Director shot and composition;
6. lighting/material evidence;
7. style;
8. adapter-specific execution choices.
